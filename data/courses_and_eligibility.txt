COER University offers a wide variety of undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programs across engineering, computer applications, management, pharmacy, and nursing. The Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) is a 4-year undergraduate program available in specializations such as Computer Science Engineering, Information Technology, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Electronics and Communication Engineering. Candidates applying for B.Tech must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry (or Computer Science) with a minimum aggregate of 45% to 50% marks.

For non-engineering programs, the Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) and Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) are 3-year courses requiring 10+2 qualification in any stream with at least 45% marks. The Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharma) requires a 10+2 completion with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology or Mathematics. For postgraduate programs, the Master of Business Administration (MBA) requires a Bachelor’s degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks, while M.Tech applicants must hold a B.Tech degree with a minimum of 50% marks or a valid GATE score.
