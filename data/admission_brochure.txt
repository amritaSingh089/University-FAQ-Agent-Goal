COER University, formerly known as College of Engineering Roorkee, was established in 1998 and is situated on the Haridwar-Roorkee Highway in Vardhmanpuram, Roorkee, Uttarakhand. It is a premier private university approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC). Spread across a green 75-acre campus, the university provides technical, professional, and management education with state-of-the-art facilities and a strong academic focus.

Admissions at COER University are based on both national-level competitive entrance examinations and merit in previous qualifying exams. For undergraduate engineering programs, national scores from JEE Main are given priority during counseling, followed by direct merit-based seats. Candidates interested in management programs like MBA can apply through CAT, MAT, XAT, or CUET PG scores. The university also offers direct admission for diploma and merit holders based on marks scored in 10+2 or equivalent board examinations.
